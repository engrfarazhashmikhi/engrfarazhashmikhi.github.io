About me

A patient, structured approach to learning Computer Science.

I'm Faraz Hashmi, a Computer Science tutor working with students online across different countries and curricula. I teach because I enjoy watching the moment something finally clicks.

Most students don't struggle with Computer Science because it's too hard — they struggle because it was explained too fast. My lessons slow down, break concepts into small pieces, and make sure you can actually use what you learn, not just repeat it.

Every lesson is one-on-one, built around your syllabus, your goals, and your pace. Whether you're writing your first line of code or preparing for a university exam, we start from where you actually are.
